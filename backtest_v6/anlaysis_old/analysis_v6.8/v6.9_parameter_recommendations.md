## Detailed Justification

### 1. `BE_ARM_R`: 0.6 → **0.4** (Highest Impact Change)

```
Current 0.6R threshold misses 1,172 STOP_HARD trades with MFE > 0:

  MFE 0.40–0.50R:  167 trades → would be SAVED at 0.4R
  MFE 0.50–0.60R:  150 trades → would be SAVED at 0.4R
                    ─────────
  Directly saved:   317 trades
  
  Current loss from these: ~₹4.7L
  At BE: ~₹0 (minus charges)
  Net improvement: ~₹4.0L
```

### 2. `TRAIL_ARM_R`: 0.8 → **0.5** + Tighter Multipliers

```
586 trades were BE-armed but never reached 0.8R trail:
  Their avg PnL: −₹46 (near zero — just eating charges)

By lowering trail to 0.5R AND tightening multipliers:
  - Trail starts 37.5% earlier
  - Tighter trail (1.6/1.3/1.0× vs 2.2/1.8/1.4×) reduces give-back
  - Current avg give-back: 0.47R → Expected: ~0.25R
  - On 1,587 trail exits: saves ~0.22R × 1,587 = ~350R units
```

### 3. `TARGET_1_MULT`: 1.5 → **1.0**

```
At 1.5R: 118 trades hit target, net PnL = +₹290 (charges eat everything)
At 1.0R: ~852 trades would touch target first (from target_stop_touch_analysis)

More trades take profit earlier → less give-back → better net PnL
The 0.5R reduction means profit is taken 33% closer to entry
```

### 4. `ENTRY_CUTOFF_TIME`: 14:05 → **12:30**

```
Time window performance:
  09:25–10:30:  +₹13,073  (33.8% WR) ← KEEP
  10:30–12:00: −₹1,17,442 (32.5% WR) ← KEEP (volume needed)
  12:00–13:15: −₹88,922   (31.6% WR) ← BORDERLINE
  13:15–14:05: −₹1,28,639  (30.2% WR) ← CUT

Cutting 13:15+ saves: ₹1.29L in losses + charges on 536 trades (₹70K)
Combined savings: ~₹1.99L
```

### 5. `MAX_CONCURRENT_POSITIONS`: 6 → **4** + Sector limit: 2 → **1**

```
At 6 positions: 3,873 trades/year → ₹5.08L charges
At 4 positions: ~2,600 trades/year (estimated) → ~₹3.4L charges
Charge savings: ~₹1.7L

PSU Bank with 2 slots: 784 trades, −₹1.51L (worst sector)
At 1 slot: ~392 trades, expected loss cut by ~₹75K
```

### 6. `BASE_RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT`: 0.005 → **0.008**

```
Current: 70.8% of trades have R < ₹2,000 → charges eat 19.5% of R
At 0.8%: Larger positions → R values ~60% higher
  Small R (currently 19.5% charge%) → drops to ~12% charge%
  
This makes the charge-to-risk ratio economically viable.
Warning: Higher risk per trade increases drawdown potential.
  Mitigated by: fewer concurrent positions (6→4) and tighter exits.
```
